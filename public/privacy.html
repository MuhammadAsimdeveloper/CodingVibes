Privacy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This notice explains how Build Vibe may process information when you use the service.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use Build Vibe, this can include your account email and password hash; project names and source files; prompts and generated outputs; build, verification and usage records; provider connection metadata; billing identifiers; audit records; and technical request information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, build and verify requested software, store project state, enforce quotas, process subscriptions, publish projects through connected providers, secure the service, prevent abuse, maintain backups, and improve reliability.

3. Connected providers

When you connect services such as GitHub, Stripe, model providers, deployment providers, media providers or isolated build infrastructure, relevant information may be transmitted to those providers to perform the requested operation. Their privacy notices and terms also apply.

4. Security

Build Vibe uses measures such as signed HTTP-only sessions, encrypted provider secrets, access controls, request throttling, isolated build runtimes and audit logging. No security measure can eliminate all risk.

5. Cookies

Build Vibe uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. The authenticated application is separated from the public marketing surface and public pages are not designed to expose project data.

6. Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and billing records, preserve audit evidence, prevent abuse, resolve disputes and satisfy applicable legal obligations. Retention may vary by data type and provider.

7. Your choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information. Use the support or contact channel presented in the application to make a request.

8. Children and changes

The service is not intended for children who are not permitted to use it under applicable law. This notice may be updated as the service changes; the effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.
